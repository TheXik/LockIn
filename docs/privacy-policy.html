Privacy Policy · Last updated March 2026

What we collect

  • Your Apple ID (for authentication only)
  • Display name and avatar emoji you choose
  • Push notification token (to send unlock request alerts)
  • Lock session data (which apps you blocked, when)

What we DON'T collect

  • Your actual app usage data — that stays on your device
  • Your contacts or phone number
  • Any data from blocked apps
  • Location, photos, or messages

How Screen Time works

LockIn uses Apple's FamilyControls framework to block apps. The actual blocking happens at the OS level on your device. We never see which specific apps you're using — we only see anonymous app tokens that Apple provides.

Data storage

Your data is stored securely on Supabase (cloud infrastructure) with row-level security. Each user can only access their own data and data shared within their pacts. We use industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest.

Data deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings. When you leave a pact, your membership data is immediately removed. When you delete your account, all your data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

Third parties

  • Apple Sign In — authentication
  • Supabase — backend database and real-time sync
  • Apple Push Notification Service — unlock request alerts

No analytics trackers, no ad networks, no data brokers. Ever.
